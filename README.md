# Ultimate High Gain DSP — experimental seed
Build: `g++ -std=c++17 -O2 test.cpp -o test && ./test`
This is a standalone prototype, not a plugin or verified amp emulation. Engine::prepare(rate), Engine::process(normalizedSample). Circuit parameters are independent of tube selection. Input calibration assumes peak-normalized digital sine amplitude. The tube current coefficients are provisional. The three-stage preamp uses approximate filters rather than a real tone stack; phase inverter is an ideal differential driver. Feedback is delayed one internal sample, transformer is reduced order, and supply is an averaged approximation. The 4x interpolation/averaging is not production anti-alias filtering. No IR convolution yet. EL84 at high voltage is hypothetical. No hardware safety claims. Tests only verify finite nonzero output for one second; startup transients and steady-state response require separate tests. Transformer primary inductance field is reserved and not used yet. No real-time performance benchmark or hardware validation performed.

## 0.2 feedback prototype
Replaces the generic feedback shelf with a backward-Euler RC divider: 100k series, 4.7k shunt, 22k rheostat and 100nF series capacitor. Assumed 4-ohm feedback tap, scaled relative to the selected load. Values follow a late-1970s-style secondary circuit analysis (https://bench.swellamps.com/preamp-mv-1978/); exact factory revision not confirmed. The Unicord scan dated 11/10/78 actually labels 6550, contrary to its extracted text. Generic 1978 Marshall drawings 78331-1 and 78331-3 are indexed at https://www.drtube.com/marshall-jmp/ but could not be inspected. Other circuit parameters remain provisional. Feedback divider ignores phase inverter loading; loop remains explicitly delayed. Isolated divider tests check high frequency attenuation; full engine smoke tests do not establish fidelity. At 100 Hz the presence endpoints can cross due to the RC transfer function. Other amplifier networks not implemented.

## 0.3 changes
Added reduced-order passive nodal tone stack, nonlinear shared-tail phase inverter, independent grid coupling capacitor states, iterative screen resistor voltage drop, transformer magnetizing current. Presence pot now 25k (reissue fallback). Feedback enters second PI grid; actual tail injection/loading remains unimplemented. Transformer and tube parameters remain provisional; PI DC bias and cathode follower are approximations. Existing smoke and isolated presence tests passed; no measured hardware validation or real-time performance certification. IR loader remains pending.

EL34 grid anchor -12 V is chosen within published range. Not full curve digitization. 6L6 resistance interpretation provisional. New implicit quasi-static primary load-line and screen resistor solve; dynamic speaker coupling still incomplete. No transformer changes on tube swap. Original full smoke run timed out; reduced solver iterations and 25ms quick smoke passed 9 combinations. Anchor and presence tests passed before iteration reduction (those modules unchanged). Full duration and realtime performance unverified. EL84 at Marshall voltages is hypothetical extrapolation. No IR or plugin wrapper yet.

## 0.5 preamp architecture prototype
Adds Preamp.h with nine selectable configurations, per-stage plate/cathode resistances, bypass and coupling capacitor states, approximate grid conduction, interstage attenuation/peaking and parameterized passive tone stack. Marshall is reference-informed, not fully verified; all other definitions are provisional engineering seeds, not verified schematics. Jose clipping is a placeholder soft limiter, not a diode circuit. Cathode follower is approximate; gain pot and bright network loading, channel switching, supply nodes and exact stage topology remain to be researched. Power amp remains the existing Marshall prototype, independent of preamp selection; native linking is not implemented. No IR loader yet. Existing oversampling and solver limitations remain. Preamp test: 0.5 seconds per model at 48 kHz, 440 Hz sine, finite nonzero output; quick integrated test: 25 ms per tube/rate combination with default Marshall. These are smoke tests, not accuracy or real-time benchmarks.

## 0.6 Jose clipping prototype
Replaces tanh with an implicit AC-coupled shunt solver before the tone stack. Modes: open, 20 V Zener pair, 12 V pair, custom asymmetric 20/12 V. 10k series, 330nF coupling and 1M nominal load; 350 ohm incremental resistance and 0.65 V forward drop are estimates. Actual tone stack loading, master pot position and transistor avalanche clipping are not solved together yet. The 12 V mode is a custom Zener alternative, not a King Kong Modern replica. Factory layout https://ceriatone.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/King-Kong-50W-Layout.pdf identifies 1N4747A and 2N5400; exact transistor connections not verified. Release build and selected quick_smoke, preamp_models, jose_clipping tests passed. Isolated 60 V peak 1 kHz input: open 59.4104, 20 V 21.9674, 12 V 14.2389, asymmetric 22.1016 V absolute peak. No hardware validation.

## 0.7 Jose extra stage
Controls::joseExtraStage selects four-stage Jose or three-stage Marshall-derived path; Controls::joseGain2 attenuates AC before the third stage in four-stage mode. Clipping remains independent. Values are provisional, gain pot loading is approximated, switching resets preamp state and may click. King Kong manual reference: https://ceriatone.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/ceriatone-king-kong-manual-100.pdf (inspiration, not a circuit transcription). Dedicated extra-stage test passed; full legacy smoke suite timed out and was not completed.

## 0.8 Jose architecture and bias
Controls expose joseArchitecture (Legacy maps old extra-stage flag, Classic, HBE, Snorkler), joseBias (Native, Warm 1.5k, Modified 4.7k, Marshall 10k, Cold 22k, Soldano 39k), joseBypass (Native, Off, Small 0.68uF, Medium 1uF, Large 22uF). Explicit bias defaults to unbypassed unless capacitor override selected. Gain 2 works in all Jose architectures. Snorkler uses four stages, provisional warm bias and 47nF coupling, 100k slope and 38k source resistance in tone stack nodal solve; other modes use 600 ohm. These are engineering approximations, not verified factory circuits. The follower remains a reduced-order approximation; plate loading is not fed back into triode or Zener solver. Switching resets states and may click. Architecture test covers 90 combinations at 48kHz with 20V clipping, finite/nonzero output only.

## 0.9 shared cold-stage controls and Rectifier consolidation
Controls.coldBias and coldBypass apply to all non-Jose prototype preamps; Jose retains its existing controls for compatibility. Native retains the current model's component values (not verified hardware values except the Marshall 10k reference). Added a custom 47k option, not a claim of a verified Mesa value. DualRectifier plus rectoVoice selects EarlyOrange, EarlyRed or MultiWattModern; legacy enum values remain accepted. The existing prototype voicings are unchanged. Switching resets preamp state and may click. Power amp linking and other Rectifier modes remain pending.

## 0.10 — Stock Marshall architecture
`JoseArchitecture::Stock` (appended to preserve existing enum values) uses the same stage specification and processing path as the standalone Marshall prototype. Gain 2 and Jose clipping are bypassed unconditionally in Stock; cold bias and cathode bypass overrides remain available. Native/Native restores the original prototype. Future UI should expose Stock under Jose/Modified Marshall and hide the standalone Marshall selection, retaining its enum for compatibility. No JUCE UI exists yet. Stock is equivalent to the existing prototype, not hardware-validated. `stock_test` compares all 35 bias/bypass combinations sample-for-sample against the standalone Marshall while deliberately setting non-default Jose clipping and Gain 2.

## 0.11 EVH family
EVH5150III selector with Blue, Red, BlueEL34, BlueStealth voices. Legacy Blue/Red IDs retained. BlueEL34 approximates reported 50W parallel coupling capacitor modification using provisional 22nF effective capacitance. BlueStealth uses provisional attenuation, 4.7nF coupling and 22uF fourth-stage cathode bypass inspired by published 100S-to-50W modification comparison. Actual input routing, resistor network and gain pot capacitor not yet transcribed. No power amp changes. Native cold bias remains existing provisional values. Sources: https://www.manualslib.com/manual/3573472/Evh-5150iii-100s.html ; https://www.scribd.com/document/485529508/EVH-5150-III-REAL-3-channel-and-stealth-mod-pptx-pdf ; https://www.rig-talk.com/forum/threads/difference-between-evh-stealth-and-evh-5150-iii-50-watt.192039/page-2 .

## 0.12
Stealth Red added to EVH family (appended enum). EVH officially documents greater channel 3 gain and expanded low control range; the provisional stage 4 attenuation .18 -> .22 and bass capacitor 22 -> 47 nF are engineering choices, not verified factory values. Reference https://www.evhgear.com/gear/amplifiers/head/5150iii-100s-head/2250250000 and 2014 service supplement https://www.manualslib.com/manual/3573472/Evh-5150iii-100s.html (full change list not retrieved).
Shared preamp improvements: implicit coupling/grid leak and approximate grid conduction, exponential cathode bypass update, gain 1 attenuates AC about tracked DC, quasi-static nonlinear unloaded ECC83 follower with 100k cathode resistor. Follower not coupled to tone stack or preceding plate; DC tracking is approximate, and actual gain pot loading remains pending. All model-specific schematic values remain as before except provisional Red Stealth. Release build and five selected CTest targets passed (preamp_models, jose_architecture, shared_modifications, stock_equivalence, evh_variants). No hardware or real-time validation.
